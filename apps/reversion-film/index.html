Reversion to the Mean

Less extreme is not the same as ordinary, and the difference is where nearly everyone lives.

Written, directed, designed and edited by Matthew Kuch, with coding support from Claude.
Data: Galton’s 1886 tally of 928 adult children; published parent-child correlations for height, earnings, wealth, schooling, IQ, lifespan and surname status; and 40 families placed as z scores against named reference populations. Every placement that is a judgement rather than a measurement carries an uncertainty band and is kept out of the headline numbers.
